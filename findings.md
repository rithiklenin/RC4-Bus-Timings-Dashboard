# RC4 Smart Display Network: Findings from Needs Survey

| Area | Key Takeaway | Implication for Dashboard Development |
|---|---|---|
| **CSC Posters / Events** | CSC is open to collaborating and integrating its publicity into the dashboard. | Reserve the **centre / largest section** for rotating CSC posters, RC4 events and sign-up deadlines. |
| **Content Rotation** | CSC raised concerns that displaying everything simultaneously may look cluttered and suggested cycling content every **1–2 minutes or longer**. | Rotate posters, events, dining information and other non-time-sensitive content instead of showing everything at once. |
| **Live Bus Timings** | Bus information is time-sensitive, particularly when residents are leaving RC4. **73%** found at-a-glance bus timings useful/extremely useful, while **80%** said they would help them decide when to leave. | Keep bus arrivals in a **smaller persistent section that remains visible throughout the content cycle**. |
| **Bus Stops to Prioritise** | Most requested: **UTown NUS Internal Shuttle Bus (80%)**, **New Town Sec Sch (76%)**, **UTown–RC4 (70%)**. Lower demand for **Aft Clementi Ave 1 (28%)** and **UTown–Cendana (25%)**. | Prioritise the top three stops in the MVP. Lower-priority stops can be hidden, collapsed or added later. |
| **RC4 Events & Announcements** | Highest-ranked content categories were **Upcoming RC4 Events (3.23)**, **Event Sign-up Deadlines (3.68)** and **RC4 Announcements (3.85)**. | Give RC4/CSC publicity high visual priority, supporting the decision to place it in the centre. |
| **Display Location** | **71%** of respondents ranked **L1** as their preferred location. | Optimise the design for L1 if deployment is approved, while ensuring it can also work at B1. |
| **Viewing Behaviour** | Around **81%** said they would look at the screen most times or every time they pass it. | Design for **quick glances**: large text, high contrast, minimal reading and no required interaction/scrolling. |
| **Clutter** | Main concerns included **too many advertisements/announcements (46%)** and **screen clutter (41%)**. | Avoid packing many widgets onto one screen. Use a clear hierarchy and rotating content. |
| **Outdated Information** | **43%** were concerned about information becoming outdated. | Automate updates where possible and show **last-updated timestamps** for live information. Expired posters/events should disappear automatically. |
| **Relevance** | **28%** were concerned that information shown might not be relevant to them. | Be selective about content rather than displaying everything available. Prioritise needs-survey results. |
| **Dining Hall Menu** | Dining information was useful but ranked below events/announcements and transport. | Keep it as a **secondary rotating component**, rather than dedicating major permanent screen space to it. |
| **Weather / Campus Updates** | Weather ranked lowest and generic campus announcements second-lowest. | **Exclude from the MVP** or only show occasionally if there is spare space. |
| **Website Backup** | Only around **20%** were likely/very likely to access the information through a website/QR code if it was not on the TV. | A website can exist as a technical fallback, but **the physical display should remain the primary product**. |
